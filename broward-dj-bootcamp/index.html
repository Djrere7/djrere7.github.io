Youth DJ Education, Built for Broward

First Spin gives Broward-area young people a supportive place to learn professional DJ skills, explore music and technology, and grow their confidence — at no cost to participating families.

Broward Family FAQ

Broward Questions & Answers

Where in Broward will classes be held?

First Spin is coming to Broward County for Summer 2027. Join the Broward interest list and you'll get the venue and schedule details first.

What ages can join in Broward?

Students ages 7 and up. A parent or guardian completes the interest list and registration.

How much does the Broward program cost?

Nothing — the summer bootcamp is offered to participating Broward families at no cost.

Do we need to bring equipment?

No. First Spin provides access to DJ equipment during class, and the program is designed for beginners.

When is the Broward 2027 season?

Between June and August 2027. Dates and registration go to our interest list first.

How do I get on the Broward list?

Complete the Broward interest form above. You'll get a welcome email and be first to hear when registration opens.
