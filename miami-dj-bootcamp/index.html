DJ Classes for Kids & Teens in Miami

A Free Youth DJ Program for Miami-Dade

First Spin is a free, hands-on summer DJ bootcamp that helps Miami-area youth build creativity, confidence, technical skills and entrepreneurial thinking — while learning from experienced DJs and mentors.

Real DJ Skills

Hands-on time on real controllers — mixing, beatmatching and building sets.

Mentorship

Guidance from professional DJs and creative-industry mentors.

Live Showcase

Every student works toward a real performance in front of family and friends.

Miami Family FAQ

Miami Questions & Answers

Where in Miami will classes be held?

First Spin is coming to Miami-Dade County for Summer 2027. Join the Miami interest list and you'll get the venue and schedule details first.

What ages can participate in Miami?

First Spin serves students ages 7 and up. A parent or guardian completes the interest list and registration.

Is the Miami program really free?

Yes. First Spin is offered to participating Miami families at no cost, sustained by donors, sponsors and community partners.

Does my child need DJ experience or equipment?

No. The Miami program is beginner-friendly and provides access to DJ equipment during class.

When does the Miami 2027 program run?

Between June and August 2027. Exact class dates and registration go to our interest list first.

How do I sign my child up in Miami?

Join the Miami interest list above. You'll receive a welcome email and be first to know when registration opens.
